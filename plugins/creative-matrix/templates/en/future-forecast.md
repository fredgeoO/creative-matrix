# How to use

Don't start with "what will the future be like"; ask "if these three things happen at once, what becomes scarce, what becomes cheap, what becomes irreversible".

Every crossing must state one trend, one counter-example, and one small move you can make in advance. At the end, sort all the small moves by reversibility and do the ones that wouldn't be fatal if wrong first.

# Tips

This table is not an oracle. Its value is preparing you early, not making you look clever.

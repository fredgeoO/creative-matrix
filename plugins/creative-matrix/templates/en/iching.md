# How to use

Both axes are the eight trigrams: the row trigram is the outer (upper) one and the column trigram the inner (lower) one, so each cell is the hexagram formed by stacking them. The plugin asks the model to reason it out; the template pre-fills none of the sixty-four hexagrams. When generating, have the model name the hexagram first (Qian above Kun is Pi; Kun above Qian is Tai), then discuss what the classic says about this situation.

Each cell must end in a concrete action — "stop adding to this investment this week", "find one person and clear the air". A reading with no action is just a story. The diagonal is the doubled trigrams (Qian above Qian); it describes where that force leads when it reinforces itself, and how to work with it.

# Tips

Here the I Ching is a tool for changing perspective. The model may occasionally stack the trigrams wrong or misname a hexagram; after loading, spot-check a few cells. For medical, legal or major financial decisions, consult the relevant professional.

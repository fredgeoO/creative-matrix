# How to use

Pick a judgment you are making and let every discipline poke at it: compounding says it is too slow, probability says the odds are thin, incentives say others will do the opposite…

If one judgment survives six lenses, it is probably not a decision held up by mood alone.

# Tips

A latticework is not about knowing a little of everything; it gives you a few extra lenses to swap in when it matters.

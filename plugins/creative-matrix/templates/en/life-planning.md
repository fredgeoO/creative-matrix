# How to use

Don't ask "what should I do with my life"; ask "at this age, under this constraint, which choice keeps the most future options open".

Every crossing needs three things: a reversible small experiment, an irreversible cost, and a fallback you can prepare in advance. Run the reversible experiments first; write the irreversible costs down and decide the next day.

# Tips

The point of this table is to leave you with less fear of "only one path left", not to compute the single right answer.

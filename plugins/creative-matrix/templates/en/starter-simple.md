# How to use

First pick an ordinary thing (an apple), then an ordinary scene (a rainy day), and ask yourself: if the apple were the only food in this rain, what would happen?

Don't aim for "advanced". The point is that every step can state its cause: because water freezes, therefore…; because a shadow follows people, therefore…. Tell the reason to a ten-year-old. If they get it, the idea stands.

# Tips

- Work one crossing at a time; don't rush to fill the whole table.
- After generating, copy down the most interesting line and continue from it next time.
- The table itself is a creative exercise you can play again and again.

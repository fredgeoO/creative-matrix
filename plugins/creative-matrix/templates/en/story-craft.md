# How to use

Pick a structure, then a genre, and make them resist each other: put three-act structure inside an urban legend — what is the second act's "confrontation"? Put the hero's journey inside a romance — is the abyss losing the other person, or losing yourself?

Each cell needs at least a one-line hook and a one-line cost. A story with a hook but no cost rarely survives three pages.

# Tips

Structure is scaffolding, not a verdict. Once you take it down, the characters still have to feel like people.

# How to use

Don't rush to "solve" the feeling. Describe it first: where the body tightens, which sentence repeats in your head, and what that sentence assumes.

Each cell's goal is not a comforting line but a small practice you can do today — "when washing dishes, just wash dishes; when the mind wanders, come back once".

# Tips

A Zen view is good for loosening attachment, not for replacing psychological or medical support. If distress keeps affecting your life, seek professional help.
